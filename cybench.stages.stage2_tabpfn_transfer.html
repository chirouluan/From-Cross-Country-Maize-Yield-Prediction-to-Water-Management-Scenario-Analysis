cybench.stages.stage2_tabpfn_transfer.prepare_cn_data module

Export leakage-safe China data for an external TabPFN continuation run.

TabPFN continuation training depends on the specific TabPFN training code and checkpoint format. This command owns the CY-Bench side of that boundary: it creates immutable train/validation tables and a provenance manifest. The trainer must write its resulting checkpoint path/hash back into that manifest.

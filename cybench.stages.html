cybench.stages.runtime module

Runtime initialization shared by GPU-backed stage entry points.

cybench.stages.runtime.initialize_torch_before_data_libraries()

Initialize CUDA PyTorch before Pandas on Windows.

Some Conda environments expose incompatible DLL search paths after NumPy or Pandas is imported. Keeping the added-directory handles alive and importing Torch first prevents c10.dll initialization failures in Qwen and TabPFN.
